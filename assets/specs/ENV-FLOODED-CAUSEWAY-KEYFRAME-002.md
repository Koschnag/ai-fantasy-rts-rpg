# ENV-FLOODED-CAUSEWAY-KEYFRAME-002

## Zweck

Originaler Kalibrierungs-Keyframe für die leise RPG-Erkundung aus spielnaher Kamera: intime Heldengruppe, Umweltgeschichte, Wegentscheidung, lokalisierbares Wunder und taktisch lesbare Geometrie. Das Bild ist eine Art-Bible-Hypothese und kein Shipping-Asset.

## Produktionsprompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original art-bible calibration keyframe for the quiet exploration phase of a cross-platform fantasy RTS/RPG
Primary request: create a completely original game-environment keyframe in which a mortal field engineer and two practical companions pause on a flooded old causeway to study a strange natural resonance; shallow water trembles in concentric lines around three fractured standing stones while abandoned repair tools, a tied empty ferry and fresh boot tracks imply that local workers disappeared only recently
Scene/backdrop: narrow valley wetland after rain, half-sunken masonry causeway, wind-bent alder trees, reed beds, distant inhabited smoke beyond a low ridge, one fork toward a dry overlook and one route through ankle-deep water
Subject: the small traveling group and the environmental clue share focus; no heroic posing and no oversized monster
Style/medium: polished stylized-realistic 3D game concept art with painterly color grading, believable but simplified stone, wet timber, cloth, leather, water and vegetation, production-feasible forms
Composition/framing: elevated three-quarter playable-camera view, closer than the strategy view but still clearly navigable; readable path fork, silhouettes separated from terrain, environmental clues legible without text
Lighting/mood: wistful, earthy, wondrous and quietly determined; overcast cool daylight breaking into one restrained warm shaft near the far inhabited smoke; localized silver-blue resonance with no neon fog
Color palette: muted peat brown, wet gray stone and desaturated green; small warm ochre on tools and clothing; very limited silver-blue anomaly accent
Materials/textures: rain-darkened stone, repaired rope, worn field equipment, rippled shallow water, sparse reeds, mud tracks and chipped hand-built masonry
Constraints: entirely original world and design; no text, no logo, no interface, no watermark; no glamorous portrait, no elaborate royal armor, no giant glowing portal, no excessive particles, no photobash artifacts; no recognizable borrowed imagery, iconic pre-existing characters, copied architecture, copied maps or familiar emblems
```
