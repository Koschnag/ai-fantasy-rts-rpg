# ENV-SLUICE-DEFENSE-KEYFRAME-003

## Zweck

Originaler Kalibrierungs-Keyframe für den Übergang zur Echtzeitstrategie: Formationslesbarkeit, zivile Aktivität, ein vom Heldenteam ausgelöster Geländehebel und kontrollierte Kampf-VFX aus weiter Spielkamera. Das Bild ist eine Art-Bible-Hypothese und kein Shipping-Asset.

## Produktionsprompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original art-bible calibration keyframe for the strategy-defense phase of a cross-platform fantasy RTS/RPG
Primary request: create a completely original wide game-environment keyframe of a working refuge defending its terraced waterworks at stormy dusk; a small field-engineer party has opened a timber sluice that redirects a shallow torrent across one approach while organized local shield lines and polearm groups hold two dry lanes; workers carry supplies and guide civilians toward warm shelter rather than disappearing from the battlefield context
Scene/backdrop: inhabited quarry basin with low practical workshops, gardens, water channels, repaired bridges and a modest palisade; three readable approach lanes, one temporarily flooded by the sluice; dark hills and rain front beyond
Subject: the combined tactical landscape is primary; approximately forty small human-scale defenders and attackers appear in clearly separated groups, with the three-person engineer party identifiable by position and restrained amber task lights rather than oversized bodies
Style/medium: polished stylized-realistic 3D game concept art with painterly color grading, believable but deliberately simplified materials, large production-feasible forms and clean unit silhouettes
Composition/framing: wide elevated three-quarter strategy camera close to intended gameplay; central defended basin, visible lane geometry and formation spacing; no cinematic close-up and no horizon-dominating creature
Lighting/mood: pressured but determined, earthy and bittersweet rather than triumphant spectacle; cool storm ambient light, warm shelter and work lights, short readable steel and dust accents, restrained amber water-control signal
Color palette: muted wet earth, dark timber and blue-gray stone; limited warm inhabited light; one functional amber interaction accent and subdued desaturated unit cloth colors
Materials/textures: rain-darkened masonry, rough timber engineering, packed mud, canvas, shallow turbulent water, worn metal and practical cloth
Constraints: entirely original world and design; units, formations, paths and the terrain lever must remain readable at 1080p; no text, logo, interface or watermark; no giant magical explosion, no glowing faction carpet, no ornate superweapons, no dense particle veil, no photobash artifacts; no recognizable borrowed imagery, iconic pre-existing characters, copied architecture, copied maps or familiar emblems
```

Ein separater Negativprompt wird nicht vorausgesetzt; sämtliche Negativbedingungen stehen nachprüfbar in `Constraints`.

## Erwartete Prüfpunkte

- drei erkennbare Anmarschwege, davon einer sichtbar durch Wasser verändert
- getrennte Einheitenformationen und zivile Arbeits-/Fluchtbewegung
- Heldenteam beeinflusst das Gelände, ersetzt aber die Armee nicht
- Kampfspannung ohne unlesbare Effektwand oder heroische Nahaufnahme
- keine Schrift, UI, Logos oder erkennbaren fremden Designs
