# MKT-RIFTWARD-RESEARCH-HERO-001

## Zweck

Originales Hero-Motiv für die öffentliche Darstellung des Forschungsprojekts. Das Motiv soll den Weg von persönlicher Erkundung über gemeinschaftlichen Aufbau bis zur lesbaren Strategiesituation in einem einzigen Bild zeigen. Es ist ein Kommunikations- und Forschungskonzept, kein Shipping-Asset und kein Beleg für vorhandenes Gameplay.

## Produktionsprompt

```text
Use case: marketing campaign hero and research-project visualization
Asset type: completely original wide cinematic key art for an experimental cross-platform fantasy RTS/RPG production, without any typography
Primary request: show one continuous wounded valley changing from intimate exploration on the left, through cooperative rebuilding in the center, into a readable defensive strategy situation on the right; three mortal field engineers follow a rain-darkened stone causeway toward an inhabited terraced quarry refuge where workers repair timber bridges, gardens and water channels, while farther right organized small formations protect civilians by opening a practical wooden sluice that redirects shallow water across one approach
Narrative idea: human-scale understanding grows into responsibility for a community and landscape; the same places, people and material systems connect all three scales rather than forming separate panels
World detail: sparse wind-bent pines and alder, weathered local stone, patched canvas, practical tools, warm workshops, a distant mismatched masonry survey tower, and a subtle geological anomaly visible only as a restrained amber mineral seam with one physically impossible shadow edge
Style/medium: polished stylized-realistic original 3D game concept art with painterly color grading, believable PBR-like materials, deliberately simplified production-feasible geometry, strong silhouettes and terrain readability suitable for a low-spec real-time game target
Composition/framing: panoramic 16:9 elevated three-quarter game camera, continuous landscape with visual flow from left foreground to warm center and right background; no triptych borders; the three scales must remain distinguishable at thumbnail size; reserve some calm dark sky and terrain space for optional external text overlay, but render no text in the image
Lighting/mood: wistful, earthy, wondrous and determined; blue-hour after rain, one dominant low sky direction, baked-light feeling, restrained warm inhabited islands, localized haze only
Color palette: about 60 percent muted earth and blue-gray stone, 30 percent desaturated forest and storm blue, 10 percent warm amber work light and anomaly accent
Constraints: entirely original clean-room world and design; no text, letters, logo, interface, watermark or mock UI; no giant hero portrait, no ornate palace, no giant monster, no neon portal, no ray-traced spectacle, no dense particle veil, no photobash artifacts; no recognizable borrowed imagery, characters, architecture, maps, symbols, faction emblems or style imitation of any named artist or existing game
```

Der Generator bietet kein separates Negativprompt-Feld; alle Negativbedingungen stehen deshalb in `Constraints`.

## Erwartete Prüfpunkte

- eine zusammenhängende Landschaft statt drei unverbundener Werbemotive
- Erkundung, Aufbau und Verteidigung bei kleiner Darstellung unterscheidbar
- Menschen, Arbeit, Wege und Geländehebel sind wichtiger als Spektakel
- große, echtzeitfähige Formen und kontrollierte Effekte
- keine Schrift, Logos, UI oder erkennbare Übernahmen
